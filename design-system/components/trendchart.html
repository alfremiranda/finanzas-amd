The last eight months, each one a stacked bar: Obligaciones · Provisiones · Gastos · Neto libre, bottom to top — the order in which a month stops being yours. 252 tall over 180 of plot; each bar takes 68% of its band. Series bind to chart/categorical/1–4 rather than to the KPI tokens directly. Same value today, and that is the point: the indirection is what stops a series drifting from the card it corresponds to. The band behind a column is the month the rest of the app is showing (chart/highlight), and clicking a bar moves the app to that month. These bars are a control, not a picture — which is why the current one has to be marked at all. Four dashed rungs on chart/grid, faint on purpose: the bars are the figure and the grid is only there when you look for it. Hover opens a Tooltip with TooltipReadout, whose swatches are readout/swatch/*, a separate rung chosen to clear 3:1 on the inverted surface — the chart colours do not (tax measures 1.44 there). On Mobile the labels drop the year. Eight «sep ’26» do not fit in 346 and nothing wraps them: they overlap. Measured on the real width, not assumed. Two series disappear for an employee profile (Obligaciones, Provisiones), leaving two. Not drawn as a variant: it is the same bar with fewer segments, and an axis for it would double the set to say nothing new. State=Hover is the tooltip: Tooltip · Side=Top with a TooltipReadout inside and one ReadoutRow per series, swatches on readout/swatch/* — a separate rung from the chart colours, chosen to clear 3:1 on the inverted surface, where the chart colours do not.
