The year month by month, each bar stacked by expense CATEGORY rather than by KPI — this is what the money was spent ON; TrendChart is what the month was made of. 220 tall over 172 of plot, twelve bands, segments in category/<name>/accent so a category is the same colour here, in its row and in category-bar. Empty months are drawn as a 4px stub on chart/empty, not left out. A year that hides its empty months compresses time and puts March next to June as though they were adjacent — and the gap IS information: it says the app was not being used yet. The code cannot draw that today. visibleData filters months without data before the branch that draws the stub, so the branch is unreachable and chart/empty has no consumer until the filter goes. That is the one behavioural change this drawing asks for. The average line sits ON TOP of the bars, dashed 4,4 at 1.5, on chart/average — six rungs above the grid because it is a figure you read against. The code draws it before the bars, so a tall stack swallows it. Its tooltip is not TooltipReadout: it is a hand-built panel with a per-category list and a «Promedio» footer. Two charts on one page with two different tooltips is a defect, and the fix belongs in the library rather than in either chart. State=Hover uses the same Tooltip · TooltipReadout as TrendChart, with a Divider row for «Promedio». That unifies the two tooltips of the page, which today are two different things. Its swatches are the CATEGORY accents, and there they fail: measured on the inverted surface they are 3.45–5.02 in Light and 1.32–1.80 in Dark. In dark mode those dots are invisible. The fix is a category swatch family fixed at the light accent — 14 tokens — and it is not minted yet, so the Dark preview shows the defect on purpose. State=Empty is the year with no expenses: title, one line of explanation and no action. The action that the Empty component brings by default is hidden — there is nothing to add from a chart. Only the TOP segment of a stack is rounded — radius/4 on its two top corners, everything below square (Alfredo, 2026-09-12): rounding every segment turns one bar into a stack of separate pills and the eye stops adding them up. The empty-month stub carries radius/4 on all four corners, which at 4px tall makes it a dash with round ends — a deliberate mark rather than a bar that was cut off.
