Navigation — Neto design system

Sin variantes—
Mobile tab bar. A floating capsule, 370×58, inset 21 from the left, right and bottom edges of the screen — the iOS 26 shape, per Apple’s HIG for tab bars. Four rules that are decisions rather than styling: Inset on three sides. The 21 all round is what makes it read as floating over the content instead of as the edge of the screen. Content scrolls underneath it; nothing else fixed may live in that band. Selected is the brand colour. Icon and label both take fg/brand — cyan/700 in Light, cyan/500 in Dark; the rest take fg/subtle. Colour is the entire selected state: no pill, no underline, no background behind the selected tab. Two to five tabs, never more, and never an action. Four are drawn here. A tab is a top-level destination you come back to; the plus lives in the FAB above the bar, not in it. Even segments. 370 minus 12 of padding each side, split four ways: 86.5 per tab, well over the 44 minimum target. Icons are 24 (Icon size=L). The label is Control/XS at 10 — Apple specifies 11, and that single point is the only place this bar knowingly departs from the HIG. On scroll the real bar minimises (tabBarMinimizeBehavior on iOS). Not drawn as a variant yet.
